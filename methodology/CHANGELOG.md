# Methodology history

Versions: one decimal for a major change (0.7 → 0.8), two decimals for a minor change (0.7 → 0.71).

- 2026-11-23 (v0.76): module `agents/declarations-of-interest.md` and `python3 -m emet.interests`. Neutral vocabulary (declaration of interest, link of interest, possible link of interest), checked by script in every field written by the agents or the engine. Relevant entities listed in the protocol (`interest_entities`, mandatory, possibly empty). Level 1: funding and declarations of each included study, from the PubMed record (`CoiStatement`, `GrantList`), Crossref funders, ClinicalTrials.gov sponsor and collaborators, open full texts and eligibility files; reviewer A on all studies, B on the dual-extraction share, adjudication of disagreements, every quotation checked verbatim. Level 2: for every author of every included study with a PubMed record, PubMed search restricted to the topic and to 36 months before or after the study; identity = same name and at least one of ORCID, institution or co-author (different ORCIDs exclude); only declarations naming a relevant entity become candidates; two independent reviewers, retained only if both agree, otherwise adjudication with `not_retained` as cautious decision. Display: badge "possible link of interest" in the engine's study tables and through `render_p0.interest_badge`, section "Declarations of interest" (sources, verbatim quotations, no amounts), declaration of the review (`review_team` in `emet.json`, included studies co-authored by its members, models used), correction channel (`interests.correction_contact`) with items hidden during examination (`corrections.json`); section `interests` of the exchange format (optional) and blocking checks in `check_review`; open data file `declarations-interets.csv`. Stages `interests-*` run after the review package; level 2b (official transparency registries) and the use in the synthesis (sensitivity analysis, GRADE) are specified but not implemented. No existing review has run the stages: rendering of existing reviews unchanged (two CSS rules added).
